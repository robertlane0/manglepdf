# Tier B — the wild corpus: sources, licences and hashes

The project's own fixtures are generated by `tools/fixturegen` from a seed. That is
exactly their weakness: every one of them is small, well-formed, and was written by
someone who already knew what the file was supposed to contain. None of them can express
the encoding problems real producers emit. The corpus here is the opposite — files that
were produced by something else, for some other purpose, and kept because they are hard.

**No PDF in this directory is committed to the repository.** They are downloaded by
`cargo xtask corpus fetch`, verified against the SHA-256 in `MANIFEST.toml`, and refused
if the hash does not match. `corpus/wild/` is git-ignored; this file and `MANIFEST.toml`
are the committed part.

## Licences

Nothing here is redistributed by this project. The licences below are recorded because
`corpus/README.md` requires that only files whose licence permits local testing are used,
and because a corpus nobody can reason about the provenance of is a corpus nobody can
maintain.


| Source | Files | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| [`mozilla/pdf.js`](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js) `test/pdfs/` | 60 | Apache-2.0 |
| [`apache/pdfbox`](https://github.com/apache/pdfbox) `pdfbox/src/test/resources/input/` | 7 | Apache-2.0 |
| NIST, IRS and the USGS | 8 | public-domain (US Government work) |
| arXiv | 2 | arXiv perpetual non-exclusive distribution licence |

In words:

- **Apache-2.0** — the test resources are part of the upstream Apache Software Foundation
  repositories and are distributed under that licence. Permits local use, modification and
  redistribution with attribution. `LICENSE.txt` in each repository is the licence text.
- **public-domain (US Government work)** — works produced by the US federal government and
  published by NIST, the IRS and the USGS. Under 17 U.S.C. §105 these are not subject to
  copyright in the United States. Note this is a *US* determination: the project may be
  built in another country, and the corpus is only ever fetched, never shipped.
- **arXiv perpetual non-exclusive distribution licence** — the two arXiv papers. Free to
  download and read; redistribution conditions apply and are not exercised here.

### Why not some other corpora

FINISH.md Gate 1 suggests "the PDF.js test corpus, the qpdf and poppler test files, the
veraPDF corpus, the Isartor suite, the Ghent output suite, GovDocs1". Of those:

- **poppler, qpdf, mupdf** test files are GPL, and several are large; the Apache-licensed
  pdf.js and pdfbox corpora cover the same producers without the licence question.
- **veraPDF and Isartor** are published under AGPL or a commercial dual licence and are
  explicitly not licensed for this use. They were not fetched.
- **Ghent Output Suite** is licensed for evaluation only. It was not fetched.
- **GovDocs1** has no licence statement that permits automated fetching at a pinned URL.
  It was not fetched.

## Known gaps

- **CAD plots** — no source with a statable licence was reachable. The maps here are
  raster topo sheets, not vector CAD output.
- **Deeply nested /Prev and /Parent chains** — the corpus has linearized and multi-revision
  files but none with a hundred-deep incremental history.
- **Very large files** — the largest is 17.6 MB. Nothing in the 100 MB class is included;
  the harness would need a page cap for those.
